Harbor

The family's day,
in one place.

Harbor is an iPhone app for the parent who already holds the calendar, the meals, and the lists.

No account is created yet. We’ll email you when a public beta is ready. Price is not set.

  • See today at a glance

    The Daily Brief, and an optional lock screen widget, show what’s next so the day is not stuck in one person’s head.

  • Share one family calendar

    Import a .ics file, or connect Google Calendar (read-only; this may require Harbor Pro). Both adults in the household can see the same plan. Outlook is not supported yet.

  • Keep the rest of the week together

    Tasks and chores, weekly meals and recipes, the grocery list, bills, subscriptions, and a simple budget live in one app.

Why I built it

I built Harbor as a stay-at-home dad of three. I wanted our household’s day to live in one place, not across texts, notes, and my own memory.

Questions

Is this iPhone only?

Yes. Harbor is an iOS app, and there is no Android version right now. It is in TestFlight beta.

Can I bring in an existing calendar?

Yes. You can import a .ics file from Life → Schedule → Manage → Sync calendars, or connect Google Calendar (read-only; this may require Harbor Pro). Harbor does not connect Outlook. To-dos, groceries, and recipes are entered by hand for now.

How much does it cost?

The price is not set yet. If you join the waitlist, we will tell you the price before anything is charged.

What about our family’s privacy?

Your household’s calendar, lists, and bills belong to your household. We do not sell your data.
